About WeatherGPT

WeatherGPT – Conversational AI for Weather & Alerts

SIH26068

WeatherGPT is an AI-powered weather assistant designed to make weather information simple, conversational, and actionable.

🎯 Our Purpose

Instead of only displaying weather data, WeatherGPT helps users understand what the weather means for them and what actions they can take.


✨ Key Features
  • 🌤️ Real-time Weather & Forecast
  • 💬 Conversational AI Weather Assistant
  • ⚠️ Weather Alerts & Warnings
  • 🎙️ Voice Input
  • 📊 Weather Trends & Charts
  • 🌧️ Detailed Precipitation
  • 🤖 AI Weather Recommendations
  • 🗺️ Interactive Weather Map
  • 🌬️ Air Quality & UV Index
  • 🔔 Smart Weather Notifications
  • 📍 Saved Locations
  • 👤 User Accounts
  • 📄 Weather Report & Export
  • 🌐 Multi-language Support

⭐ What Makes WeatherGPT Different?

Traditional weather apps mainly tell users what the weather is. WeatherGPT helps users understand what the weather means for them.

It combines real-time weather data, location context, forecasts, alerts, and AI conversation to provide simple, contextual, and actionable weather insights.


🛠️ Technology
  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap
  • Backend: Python, Flask
  • AI: Llama-based AI
  • Weather Data: Open-Meteo API
  • Database: SQLite
  • Voice: Web Speech API
